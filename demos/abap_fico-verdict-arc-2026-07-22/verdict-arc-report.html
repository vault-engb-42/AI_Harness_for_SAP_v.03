Four gates fire, each for a different real reason

None is a duplicate of another. Two are defects the generator can fix; two need a human.

atc-p1-nonzero

11 priority-1 residuals in the generated artifacts — RAP handler discipline and a SELECT in a loop.

atc-p2-nonzero

618 priority-2. Under C3 both tiers hard-block, because SAP blocks transport on both.

auth-delta-unattested

The authorization footprint moved: 0 → 10 AUTHORITY-CHECKs, 0 → 3 BDEF authorization clauses. Not a loss — but L7 says a reviewer signs first.

parity-not-equivalent:needs_review

An imperative → declarative rewrite cannot be scored structurally, so it goes to a human rather than being asserted equivalent.

And it self-corrects. The driver classifies the ATC residuals as defects, regenerates with the findings threaded in, and bumps the cycle counter so the loop is bounded. The owed attestations do not stall it — a defect outranks an attestation, so the artifact is fixed first and attested after.

Eight of the fourteen were false passes — an authorization loss reaching a human labelled clean. Two (#9, #10) were the inverse: the single most canonical rewrite this harness exists to perform could not pass offline at all.

Defects 13 and 14 share a root cause worth keeping: every DCL and BDEF fixture in the suite was hand-named in memory, so the tests agreed with each other about a filename convention the real corpus does not follow. A green suite could not have caught either — only loading from disk did.

Where they came from

  • 1–11 — a five-lens adversarial review of the arc: 123 agents, 39 raw findings, 12 after dedupe. Eleven survived verification and each was re-probed by hand before any code changed. One was rejected: the positional-blind multiset in changedLines is a documented design choice, not a defect — a moved-but-identical line's warning is carried debt and must not count.
  • 12 — found by asking what actually calls the arc. Nothing did.
  • 13 — found the moment #12 was wired end-to-end and a real directory was loaded.
  • 14 — found by this demo's own run, against real corpus filenames.

Defects 1 and 6 were the exact risk surfaces recorded in the handoff for an adversarial pass that was skipped. Skipping it let two false-pass defects propagate through two further boxes into the verdict.

Same corpus, same generated source as abap_fico-e2e-2026-07-14 — deliberately.
